01 | PROBLEM STATEMENT

The Problem Statement

The structural disconnect between macro financial news and the everyday Indian household's monthly budget.

CENTRAL CHALLENGE

"Global macroeconomic shocks ripple across international supply chains and domestic financial markets, yet Indian citizens, retail investors, and financial planners lack an evidence-backed mechanism to trace how headline disruptions translate directly into household expenses and monthly SIP affordability."

CHALLENGE 01
Fragmented Jargon & Isolated News
Headlines like "Brent surges 12%" or "Fed hikes 50 bps" are presented in silos, leaving everyday citizens unable to connect macro events to daily living costs.
CHALLENGE 02
Black-Box Generative AI Hallucinations
Standard LLMs fabricate financial figures without source citations, verified elasticity models, or mathematical boundaries, destroying institutional credibility.
CHALLENGE 03
Missing Micro-Level Transmission
Existing financial terminals stop at equity indices (Nifty, Sensex), failing to bridge the multi-hop propagation into freight, food inflation, and regional household bills.
CHALLENGE 04
Compounding Wealth Erosion
When inflation strikes, households panic and pause their Systematic Investment Plans (SIPs), causing irreversible long-term wealth destruction from lack of quantified alternatives.
THE GAP
The Indian financial ecosystem lacks a dual-engine architecture that pairs transparent NLP news ingestion with a deterministic, official-data-calibrated input-output graph backed by verifiable source receipts (MoSPI HCES 2023–24).

05 | METHODOLOGY & DATA RIGOR

Empirical Foundations & Formula Verification

Grounding macroeconomic estimates in peer-reviewed econometric literature and official Government of India surveys.

THE CORE FORMULA

Household Transmission Calibration

ΔExpense_i = MPCE_baseline × w_i × (ΔP_shock × η_i) × (1 - β_policy)
Parameter Legend:
• MPCE_baseline: Monthly Per Capita Consumer Expenditure (MoSPI HCES 2023–24).
• w_i: Budget weight of commodity category i (Food 28%, Fuel 19.4%, Utilities 19.5%).
• ΔP_shock: Percentage shock extracted from verified source news (+12.0% Crude).
• η_i: Empirical pass-through elasticity factor (OMC diesel pricing, logistics freight).
• β_policy: State/Central fiscal cushion (Excise duty buffer, LPG subsidy).
GOVERNMENT DATA REGISTERS

Audited Benchmark Foundations

  • MoSPI HCES 2023–24 Factsheet: Published August 2024. Baseline MPCE for Madhya Pradesh (Urban: ₹5,200; Rural: ₹3,179).
  • PPAC (Petroleum Planning & Analysis Cell): Historical fuel price build-ups, crude import parity, and OMC margin buffers.
  • RBI & MoCI WPI-to-CPI Bulletins: Empirical transmission lag models (14–21 day wholesale-to-retail freight pass-through).
  • NSSO 68th Round Benchmark: Elasticity coefficients for cereal, edible oil, and vegetables.

MODEL BOUNDARIES & LIMITATIONS

What the Model Does Not Do

  • No Stock Price Prediction: We map input cost shocks, not equity market speculative bets.
  • Supply Contract Absorption Lag: Real-world commercial contracts delay price transmission by 14–30 days; models reflect equilibrium steady state.
  • Unannounced Policy Interventions: Sudden excise duty cuts or export bans alter elasticity chains; captured via explicit caveats in receipts.
